    SentinelNode / Recorded notification delivery

    One notification. Two requests.

    A receiver can accept a notification and lose its reply. Sentinel retries the same reference; the receiver recognizes it. These six recorded steps used real loopback HTTP requests and real retry due times. They are separate from the incident replay above.

    Queued and acknowledged notifications at each recorded step. The last column compares unique receiver notifications with accepted requests.
    Recorded stepQueuedAcknowledgedUnique / Requests
    Healthy baseline000 / 0
    Receiver unavailable300 / 0
    Receiver restored033 / 3
    Reply lost after acceptance134 / 4
    Same reference retried044 / 5
    Confirmed recovery055 / 6

    Three events wait while the receiver is unavailable, even though services have recovered. The lost-reply retry then raises accepted requests from four to five while unique notifications stay at four. The final recovery is a fifth notification. Receiver acknowledgements do not establish email delivery or live AWS operation.
